UK solar & heat pump data, area by area

Is solar worth it where you live?

Most solar and heat pump advice is national guesswork. We're building a page for every UK local authority, using official data on how much sunshine your area actually gets, how many neighbours have already installed, and how much grant money is being claimed locally.

What each local page will show

1

Real solar output

Estimated yearly generation per kW of panels for your area, from the EU's PVGIS solar model.

2

Local uptake

How many certified solar and heat pump installations have gone in near you, from MCS installation data.

3

Grants claimed

Boiler Upgrade Scheme vouchers issued in your area, from official government statistics.

4

A straight answer

Rough payback maths you can check yourself, plus a route to compare quotes from certified installers.

Launching soon. Local area pages are being rolled out across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. Every figure links back to its public source. See our methodology.
